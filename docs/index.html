Questions

Good to know

Does my data leave my computer?

No. Capture, distillation, embeddings, and search all run inside your browser. The only network request is a one-time ~25 MB download of the local embedding model. Your messages and memories are never transmitted anywhere.

How does it know what to remember?

Smriti reads your own messages and extracts durable, first-person facts — your role, your projects, your decisions, your preferences — while skipping one-off requests and chit-chat. Everything is editable: pin what matters, delete what doesn't.

Which AI tools are supported?

Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini today, with more on the way. Crucially, memory is shared across all of them — something you established in one shows up in the others.

Do I have to take notes or tag things?

No. Memory builds itself from how you already use AI. You can also teach it a fact manually anytime.
